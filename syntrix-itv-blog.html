Syntrix at iTV

Syntrix featured on “Thurunu” iTV program

Admin

November 24, 2025 • 1 min read

Introduction

We are excited to share a proud milestone in the journey of Syntrix. Recently, Syntrix was featured on “Thurunu”, a television program aired on iTV that highlights young entrepreneurs and emerging innovators across Sri Lanka.

“Thurunu” serves as a national platform that showcases innovative ideas, startup journeys, and impactful solutions developed by youth who are shaping the future. Being selected to appear on this program is a meaningful recognition of Syntrix’s vision and its potential contribution to sustainable and technology-driven solutions.

During the interview session, the discussion focused on the problem Syntrix aims to address, the motivation behind developing the solution, and how technology can be used to create practical impact for businesses and communities. The feature also provided an opportunity to share our long-term vision and the role Syntrix hopes to play in driving smarter, more sustainable systems.

The episode featuring Syntrix is scheduled to be telecast soon on iTV. We will share the exact broadcast details once they are officially announced.

We extend our sincere gratitude to the Thurun iTV team for the opportunity and encouragement given to young innovators. This recognition strengthens our commitment to continue building, improving, and scaling Syntrix.

Stay tuned for the upcoming telecast and further updates on our journey.
